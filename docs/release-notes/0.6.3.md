Quitting is safe and always finishes.

- Quit warns when agents are working, and Restart to Update can wait until they are idle.
- Quitting always finishes, even after the menu bar menu was opened.
- The menu bar counts agents that are asking as well as working.
- A Run pane stopped by Quit reads as stopped, not failed, for you and your teammates.
- A failed update check says why and keeps the last good check time; Merge All Ready says which child it skipped.
