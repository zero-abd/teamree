A new look, and a place to land what your agents finish.

- New look: Studio and Studio Light themes with live previews in Settings, new icons, and a redesigned sidebar, palette and dialogs.
- Every split has its own tab bar right above it.
- Changes is a source-control view: Staged and Changes sections, List or Tree, and a commit box with Commit & Push and Amend.
- Ended and failed agents are cards that say when they ended; Resume continues the conversation, and Resume All brings back every stopped agent.
- An agent's question is a card at the top of its pane, with its options as buttons and Reply….
- Review a teammate's finished task and send line comments back; Request Review asks a teammate to review yours.
- Ready to land lists finished tasks with Review, Land and Discard; Land All lands them in order, fetching origin first and stopping at a conflict.
- A commit a hook refuses names the hook, with Details and Send to Agent.
